How to play

Tips and mechanics for Classroom.io

Getting started

  1. Open Classroom.io in your browser.
  2. You take a seat right away. On CrazyGames your account username is used automatically; guests get a name like Student_0192.
  3. Customize your avatar (skin, hair, shirt) any time in Settings.
  4. Earn brainpower by clicking Take notes! or by buying generators.

Brainpower & generators

Brainpower (BP) is the main currency. You earn it by taking notes manually (click or spacebar) and from generators in the school kiosk. Each generator produces BP per second automatically. New tiers unlock once you own at least one of the previous tier.

The nine generators:

  • Stubby Pencil — your first helper
  • Sticky Notes — sticks to every solution
  • Calculator — works in the background
  • Study Group — four brains, one goal
  • Cheat Sheet — top secret
  • Espresso Machine — under the desk
  • Homework Bot — works while you sleep
  • Time-Turner — homework in three timelines
  • Galaxy Brain — the endgame

Upgrades & buying

In the school kiosk you can set buy quantity to 1, 10, or Max. Upgrades double production for certain generators or boost your click power. Watch the requirements — some upgrades need a minimum number of generators or clicks.

Paper airplanes & stealing

Click another player’s desk for three throws. Paper airplanes (45 s) steal a slice of unspent brainpower. Spitballs are weaker but come back in about 12 s. Ink blots cut the victim’s production in half for 20 s. Use Look busy on your own desk to bounce airplanes and ink for 20 s. If someone hits you, revenge against them is ready in 10 s. During a teacher patrol, airplanes and ink are risky: a 50% chance of detention (90 seconds at 25% production).

Room events

  • Pop quiz: Solve the math question quickly — correct answers grant bonus BP and a ×2 multiplier for two minutes.
  • Teacher patrol: Airplanes and ink are dangerous — high risk when throwing.
  • Recess riot: For 30 seconds, throws recharge almost immediately.
  • Substitute period: Everyone produces twice as much for a while.

Class goal & graduating

Everyone in the room works toward a shared class goal. Reaching it grants a strong group bonus. With enough brainpower you can Graduate (prestige): you start over with gold stars that permanently boost production.

Attendance, homework & desk skins

Open School day (calendar icon in the header) to stamp in for a daily attendance bonus. Consecutive days grow a streak that pays more brainpower and unlocks desk colors the whole class can see. Miss a day and you can watch a rewarded ad for a late slip. Each day also has three homework tasks (take notes, buy something, and either throw a paper airplane or join a pop quiz). Turn them all in for a notebook bonus.

Chat, emotes & leaderboard

Bottom-left is chat for short messages and emotes. Messages run through a profanity filter. The trophy icon in the header opens the chalkboard leaderboard. In Settings you can change language, avatar and audio.

Boss key

Press Tab to disguise the game as a math notes document. Press Tab again or click Close to return to the classroom.

Offline progress

When you leave or close the page, your generators keep working for up to eight hours. When you return, you receive the collected brainpower as a welcome bonus.
