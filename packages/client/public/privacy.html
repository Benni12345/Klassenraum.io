Privacy policy

How Classroom.io processes data

Last updated: July 2026

1. Controller

The controller for data processing on this website is the operator of Classroom.io. Contact details are in the legal notice.

2. What data we process

When you play Classroom.io, the following data may be processed:

  • Player profile: chosen name, avatar settings, game progress (brainpower, generators, upgrades, stats)
  • Technical data: connection information for real-time multiplayer (WebSocket)
  • Chat messages: texts you send in the in-game chat (shown while the session is active; not permanently archived)
  • Local storage: an anonymous guest token and UI preferences (language, music/SFX, tutorial state) in your browser (localStorage). Preferences are written at most once every 30 seconds. Game progress itself is not stored in localStorage — it lives on our game server.
  • CrazyGames account (when applicable): if you play on CrazyGames and sign in, progress is linked to your CrazyGames user ID and username via their SDK (JWT verified on our server). Guest progress is copied into a new CrazyGames-linked account once; later logins resume that account without re-importing guest data.

We do not require email/password registration. On CrazyGames, logged-in players use their CrazyGames username automatically; guests get a stylized name like Student_0192. A play account is created automatically on first visit.

3. Purpose of processing

Data is used solely to:

  • provide the multiplayer game and store your progress,
  • show your name and avatar to other players in the classroom,
  • maintain technical stability and security of the service.

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract / pre-contractual steps — providing the game) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating and securing the service).

5. Retention

Game progress is stored on the server while the account remains active. Inactive accounts may be deleted after a longer period of inactivity. You can remove the local token anytime via your browser settings.

6. Advertising

When Classroom.io runs on CrazyGames, ads are served only through the CrazyGames SDK according to their platform rules. On the standalone website, Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) may be used to show ads.

Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads based on prior visits to this or other sites. Data such as IP address, device information, and ad interactions may be processed.

You can opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings, aboutads.info (US), or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).

More about how Google uses data: Google Partner Sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Cookies & local storage

Besides any ad cookies set by partners, Classroom.io uses localStorage in the browser to store your anonymous play account. This is technically necessary for the game and is not a tracking cookie.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise these rights, contact the address in the legal notice. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Hosting

The website is hosted by third-party providers (e.g. Vercel, Fly.io, CrazyGames CDN). Technical access data (IP address, timestamps) may appear in server logs, usually kept briefly for operations.

10. Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the service or legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
