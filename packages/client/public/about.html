About Classroom.io

A free multiplayer idle game for the browser

What is Classroom.io?

Classroom.io is a free pixel-art browser game. Unlike classic idle games, every player sits in the same classroom — live, at the same time, at their own desks. You collect brainpower (BP), buy school-themed upgrades from a stubby pencil to a galaxy brain, and steal points from classmates with paper airplanes.

The game runs entirely in the browser — no download or install. It works for short breaks and longer sessions alike, with offline progress for up to eight hours when you come back.

Why one shared classroom?

The shared room makes Classroom.io a social idle game. You see other players at their desks, follow the chalkboard leaderboard, and work together on the class goal. Room events like pop quizzes, teacher patrols, and substitute periods affect everyone at once and keep things lively.

Core features

  • Idle economy: Nine school-themed generators, upgrades, and prestige (“Graduate” for gold stars)
  • Multiplayer: Live desks, chat (passed notes), emotes, and a leaderboard
  • Paper airplanes: Steal brainpower from others — with cooldowns and risk during teacher patrol
  • Room events: Pop quiz, teacher patrol, and substitute period with bonus or penalty effects
  • Boss key: Press Tab to disguise the game as a math notes page

Who is it for?

Classroom.io is for anyone who likes short, relaxed multiplayer games in the browser — especially students who want a light idle game between classes. The default language is English; a German translation is available in Settings.

Tech & fairness

Important game actions — clicks, purchases, steals, and production — are validated on the server so play stays fair with many people online. The client draws the pixel scene on a canvas; the UI (shop, chat, settings) is a normal web page.
